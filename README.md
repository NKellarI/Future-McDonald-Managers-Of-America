What is the software product?
A grocery price comparison app that lets users create a shopping list, compare prices for those items across different grocery stores, and estimate the total cost of the list at each store.

Possible names considered:
- Smart Shopper
- Cart Compare

Final name chosen:
Smart Shopper

Potential customers, end users, or buyers:
- College students
- Families trying to lower grocery costs
- Budget conscious people
- People who shop at multiple grocery stores
- Anyone wanting to save time and money when grocery shopping

Potential features and functions:- Create and save grocery shopping lists
- Search for grocery items and compare prices between stores
- Calculate the estimated total cost of a shopping list at each store
- Show which store has the lowest overall total
- Display individual item price differences
