One list. Every price. More savings. To make grocery shopping simpler and more affordable by helping shoppers quickly compare prices across stores and make informed decisions about where to shop.

Key Features
  • Create and organize your shopping list
  • Compare item prices across different stores
  • Find the cheapest store for your entire list
  • Calculate and compare your total grocery cost
  • See how much money you could save
  • Compare multiple stores in one place
  • Make smarter shopping decisions
