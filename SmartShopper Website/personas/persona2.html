Smart Shopper All Personas

Jordan Lee

The Family Grocery Planner

Background & Personal Context

Jordan, age 38, is a full-time high school social studies teacher and the parent of three children aged 5, 9, and 12. Jordan holds a master's degree in education, and their spouse works full-time as a physical therapist. With three growing kids involved in after-school sports, the household goes through large quantities of staple grocery items every week and often has 20 to 30 items on a shopping list. Rising food costs have put pressure on their family budget, motivating Jordan to lower their weekly grocery bills without sacrificing meal quality.

Education & Technology Experience

Jordan has average-to-good technical skills and is comfortable using mobile apps, grading software, online shopping, and digital calendars, though Jordan dislikes overly complicated interfaces that waste time. Jordan usually shops on Sunday mornings and wants a streamlined system that remembers the family's recurring grocery list, calculates the estimated total cost across different stores, and highlights potential savings.

Goals

  • Plan large 20–30 item family grocery trips efficiently.
  • Reduce weekly household grocery expenses.
  • Avoid forgetting essential household items.
  • Quickly compare complete shopping baskets across stores.

Needs

  • Support for large shopping lists.
  • Prices from multiple grocery stores in one view.
  • Estimated totals for the entire shopping list and savings totals.
  • Easy quantity selection that automatically updates the total.

Frustrations

  • Rewriting large paper shopping lists from scratch every week.
  • Keeping track of multi-item quantities manually.
  • Calculating the total cost of a large shopping trip across stores.
"With a big grocery trip, I care more about the total cost than the price of just one item."
