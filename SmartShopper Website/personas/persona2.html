Smart Shopper All Personas

Jordan Lee

The Family Grocery Planner

Background

Jordan is a 38-year-old full-time employee and parent. Jordan regularly purchases groceries for a household and often has 20–30 items on a shopping list.

Goals

  • Plan large grocery trips.
  • Reduce household expenses.
  • Avoid forgetting items.
  • Quickly compare complete shopping baskets.

Needs

  • Large shopping lists.
  • Prices from multiple grocery stores.
  • Estimated totals for his entire shopping list and saving totals.
  • Quantity selection for grocery items.

Frustrations

  • Keeping track of quantities.
  • Manually calculating grocery costs.
  • Calculating the total cost of a large shopping trip.

Technology Experience

Jordan is comfortable using mobile apps, online shopping, and digital calendars.

"With a big grocery trip, I care more about the total cost than the price of just one item."
