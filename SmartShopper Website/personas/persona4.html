Smart Shopper All Personas

Sam Carter

Convenience-Focused Shopper

Background & Personal Context

Sam, age 27, is a freelance graphic designer and web developer who works from a home studio. Sam holds a bachelor's degree in digital media and manages multiple client projects on tight deadlines. Outside of work, Sam focuses on fitness and nutrition, preparing high-protein lunches and dinners in bulk every Sunday and making quick restock trips mid-week. Because Sam values billable work time as much as money, Sam wants a fast, frictionless way to compare grocery costs without spending an hour planning.

Education & Technology Experience

As a web developer and power user of digital technology, Sam expects fast search functionality, clean data visualization, and smart automation. Previously, Sam tried maintaining a custom spreadsheet to track recurring grocery prices across stores, but manually updating prices became too tedious. Sam wants to use Smart Shopper to quickly create short restock lists or reopen saved meal-prep lists, compare nearby stores in seconds, and easily understand the total and unit cost results.

Goals

  • Build or reopen grocery lists in under two minutes.
  • Quickly compare store totals without checking multiple apps.
  • Balance grocery savings against travel distance and time.
  • Understand unit and package price differences at a glance.

Needs

  • Fast item search with auto-complete and saved list duplication.
  • Instant store total rankings and clear savings summaries.
  • Distance and store selection filters for quick mid-week trips.
  • Clear breakdowns of package sizes and unit prices.

Frustrations

  • Wasting billable work time manually comparing store prices.
  • Apps that require too many taps to build a short 5-item list.
  • Confusing package size differences across competing grocery chains.
"I want to open my saved list, tap compare, and know in ten seconds which nearby store saves me the most money."
