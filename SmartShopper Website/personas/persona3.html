Smart Shopper All Personas

Alex Morgan

Multi-Store Deal Seeker

Background & Personal Context

Alex, age 64, is a retired bookkeeper living on a fixed pension income. Alex holds an associate degree in accounting and spent nearly 40 years managing ledgers for a local hardware distributor. Enjoying cooking from scratch and baking for family on weekends, Alex is highly price-sensitive and has historically spent hours every Wednesday comparing printed weekly newspaper circulars and coupons from multiple grocery chains to get the lowest price on every item.

Education & Technology Experience

Alex uses a tablet and smartphone daily for email, recipes, and family photos, but dislikes cluttered store apps with tiny text, pop-up ads, or confusing digital coupon menus. Because several local grocery chains have phased out printed paper ads, Alex wants to use Smart Shopper as a clean, easy-to-read replacement to audit individual item price differences across stores, see when prices were last updated, and decide whether splitting a trip across two nearby stores is worth the savings.

Goals

  • Find the lowest individual item prices across local grocery stores.
  • Evaluate whether shopping at multiple stores saves enough money.
  • Replace paper circulars with a clear digital price comparison tool.
  • Verify that displayed store prices are current and reliable.

Needs

  • Side-by-side individual item price difference breakdowns.
  • Clear visual indicators showing the cheapest store per item.
  • Price-update timestamps showing how current the data is.
  • An uncluttered, readable checklist mode for in-store use.

Frustrations

  • Local grocery stores discontinuing printed weekly circulars.
  • Cluttered grocery store apps that require separate logins.
  • Overpaying for expensive pantry staples like coffee, olive oil, or baking supplies.
"If one store has cheaper meat and the store across the street has cheaper pantry staples, I want to see the exact price differences so I can get the best deal on both."
