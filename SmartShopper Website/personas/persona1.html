Smart Shopper All Personas

Maya Rodriguez

Budget-Conscious Student

Background

Maya is a 21-year-old college student who works part-time while attending school. She is comfortable using smartphones and websites but has a limited grocery budget.

Goals

  • Stay within her weekly grocery budget.
  • Find the lowest prices on grocery items.
  • Spend less time comparing stores manually.
  • Avoid unexpected grocery expenses.

Needs

  • A simple shopping list builder.
  • Prices from multiple grocery stores.
  • Estimated totals for her entire shopping list.
  • Quantity selection for grocery items.

Frustrations

  • Having to visit multiple store websites.
  • Manually calculating grocery costs.
  • Finding out the total is higher than expected at checkout.

Technology Experience

Maya is comfortable using mobile applications, online shopping websites, and digital payment services.

"I want to know where my whole grocery list will cost the least before I go shopping."
