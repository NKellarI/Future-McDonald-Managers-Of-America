Smart Shopper All Personas

Maya Rodriguez

Budget-Conscious Student

Background & Personal Context

Maya, age 21, is a junior majoring in Nursing at Bellarmine University. She grew up in a middle-class household in Indiana and currently lives in an off-campus apartment with three roommates. In addition to a full course load and clinical rotations, she works 15 hours a week part-time as a barista to pay for her rent, gas, and groceries. Because she is paying her own way through school, she tracks every dollar she spends and tries to keep her weekly grocery bill under $45.

Education & Technology Experience

As a third-year nursing student and digital native, Maya is highly comfortable using smartphones, mobile applications, online shopping websites, campus software, and digital payment services. However, between classes, studying, and work shifts, she does not have time to check multiple grocery store websites or apps to see who has the cheapest prices. She wants to use Smart Shopper to quickly build her weekly list, compare total cart costs across nearby Walmart, Kroger, and Target locations, and immediately see which single store will save her the most money.

Goals

  • Stay within her weekly grocery budget.
  • Find the lowest prices on staple grocery items.
  • Spend less time comparing stores manually.
  • Avoid unexpected grocery expenses at checkout.

Needs

  • A simple shopping list builder with saved lists.
  • Prices from multiple grocery stores.
  • Estimated totals for her entire shopping list.
  • Quantity selection and item swap options.

Frustrations

  • Having to visit multiple store websites or apps.
  • Manually calculating grocery costs.
  • Finding out the total is higher than expected at checkout.
"I want to know where my whole grocery list will cost the least before I go shopping."
