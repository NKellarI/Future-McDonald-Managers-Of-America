Interview 2

Family Grocery Shopper

Understanding household grocery planning and budgeting.

Interview Information

Date: October 10, 2026

Interviewer: Tristan Hibbard

Interviewee: Tristan's Mother

Duration: 20 minutes

Interview Type: In-Person

Product: Smart Shopper

Interview Overview

The purpose of this interview was to understand how someone who purchases groceries for a household plans shopping trips and compares prices.

The interviewer introduced Smart Shopper and explained how users would be able to enter grocery items, select stores, compare prices, and view estimated totals for their shopping lists.

Questions and Responses

I usually shop once or twice a week. I make a list before going because I am buying groceries for the whole family. I usually write down everything we need.

Keeping track of everything and figuring out how much the entire trip will cost. With a larger shopping list, it is difficult to calculate everything manually.

Definitely. I would rather know which store has the cheapest total instead of looking at individual sales and trying to figure out which store is better overall.

Yes. Having categories like dairy, produce, meat, and household products would make a large list easier to manage.

Yes. If I change the amount of something I need, I would want the total to update automatically.

It would be helpful to see how much money I could save by choosing one store over another. I would also want to know when the prices were last updated.

Key Findings

  • The user regularly creates large grocery lists for a household.
  • Comparing complete shopping totals is more useful than comparing individual items alone.
  • Categories would make larger shopping lists easier to navigate.
  • Automatic recalculation is important when quantities or items change.
  • Showing potential savings would help users make shopping decisions.
  • Price-update information would help users understand the reliability of estimates.

Product Requirements Identified

  1. Support large shopping lists.
  2. Allow users to organize items into categories.
  3. Allow users to enter and modify item quantities.
  4. Automatically recalculate estimated store totals.
  5. Display potential savings between stores.
  6. Display price-update information.

Conclusion

The interview suggests that families and household grocery planners would benefit from Smart Shopper's ability to compare the total cost of a large shopping list. Features such as quantity management, categories, automatic calculations, and savings comparisons would help make grocery planning more efficient.
