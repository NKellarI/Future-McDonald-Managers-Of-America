Interview 1

Budget-Conscious College Student

Understanding grocery shopping on a student budget.

Interview Information

Date: October 8, 2026

Interviewer: Tristan Hibbard

Interviewee: A Friend of Tristan's

Duration: 15 minutes

Interview Type: In-Person

Product: Smart Shopper

Interview Overview

The purpose of this interview was to understand how a college student manages grocery expenses and what features would be useful in a grocery price comparison application.

The interviewer explained the Smart Shopper concept, which allows users to create a grocery shopping list, compare item prices between stores, and view estimated shopping totals.

Questions and Responses

I usually make a list on my phone or remember what I need. I normally shop at one store because comparing prices takes too much time.

Not very often. I might check a sale online, but I don't normally compare every item because it is inconvenient.

I would like to enter my grocery list and immediately see which store has the cheapest total. That would save me time and money.

Yes. Sometimes I need multiple of the same item, and the total should account for that.

Yes. I tend to buy many of the same groceries every week, so having a saved list would be helpful.

The biggest concern would be whether the prices are accurate. If the prices are outdated, the estimated total might be misleading.

Key Findings

  • The user wants to compare the total cost of an entire shopping list.
  • Quantity support is important for accurate estimates.
  • Saved shopping lists would improve convenience.
  • Price accuracy and update information are important.
  • The application should be simple and quick to use.

Product Requirements Identified

  1. Shopping list creation and editing.
  2. Store-by-store price comparison.
  3. Automatic estimated total calculation.
  4. Quantity selection for grocery items.
  5. Saved shopping lists.
  6. Clear information explaining that prices are estimates.

Conclusion

The interview suggests that budget-conscious college students could benefit from Smart Shopper because it would reduce the time spent manually comparing grocery prices. The most important features for this user are shopping list management, estimated store totals, and accurate price information.
