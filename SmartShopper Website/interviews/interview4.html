Interview 4

Multi-Store Deal Seeker on a Low Budget

Understanding how a budget-conscious shopper hunts for deals across multiple grocery chains.

Interview Information

Date: October 14, 2026

Interviewer: Jayden West

Interviewee: A Friend of Jayden's

Duration: 15 minutes

Interview Type: In-Person

Product: Smart Shopper

Interview Overview

The purpose of this interview was to learn how a shopper on a strict budget compares individual item prices across multiple grocery stores and decides whether to visit more than one store to get the best deals.

The interviewer introduced Smart Shopper and explained how the app compares item prices and overall totals across stores like Walmart, Kroger, and Target so users can see exactly how much they save.

Questions and Responses

Because my budget is super tight, I don't just buy everything at one place. I usually check Walmart, Kroger, and Aldi on my phone to see who has the cheapest chicken, eggs, rice, and produce that week, and sometimes I hit two stores that are close by to stretch my budget.

Switching back and forth between three different apps is exhausting. I have to search for every single item multiple times and try to remember which store had the cheaper price, or write the prices down in my notes app.

Having one list that automatically pulls up the prices for all the stores side by side would save me at least a ton of time every week. I could immediately see if one store is cheapest overall, or which individual items are way cheaper at another store.

If going to a second store only saves me a dollar or two, it's not worth the gas. But if Kroger has a huge sale on meat and Walmart is $6 cheaper on all my canned and dry goods, I'll definitely stop at both. Seeing the exact dollar savings in the app would make that decision super easy.

I start looking for items I can swap for generic store brands, or I take non essential snacks off my list until the total is back under my limit. It would be awesome if the app let me swap an item for a cheaper brand and updated the total right away.

Comparing store brand items fairly across stores is really important because people on a low budget rarely buy name brands. Also, seeing the price per ounce helps when one store has a slightly different package size than another.

Key Findings

  • Low-budget shoppers actively compare prices across multiple stores but waste time switching between separate store apps.
  • Highlighting the cheapest store for each individual item helps deal seekers spot major price gaps.
  • Showing the exact dollar savings helps users decide if visiting a second store is worth the trip.
  • Users on a strict budget need to easily swap expensive items for cheaper store-brand alternatives to stay under their cap.
  • Matching equivalent generic/store-brand items across chains is essential for budget shoppers.

Product Requirements Identified

  1. Side-by-side item price comparison across multiple stores.
  2. Visual highlighting of the lowest price for each individual item.
  3. Display of total dollar savings between stores and split-trip options.
  4. Support for store-brand product comparisons and quick item swaps.
  5. Immediate recalculation of the cart total when items are swapped or removed.
  6. Unit price (per ounce/pound) display for comparing different package sizes.

Conclusion

This interview confirms that low-budget deal seekers are prime users for Smart Shopper. By eliminating the need to jump between multiple store apps and clearly showing both individual item price differences and total basket savings, the app empowers budget shoppers to maximize every dollar.
