13. Quickly Creating a Short List

Persona: Sam Carter - Convenience-Focused Shopper

Overall Objective

Build a quick 5-item restock grocery list in under a minute between freelance client meetings.

Scenario Narrative

On a busy Wednesday afternoon with a web design client deadline approaching, Sam realizes the kitchen is out of five essentials: milk, eggs, bread, chicken, and bananas. Not wanting to waste billable work time, Sam opens Smart Shopper and uses the instant auto-complete search bar to tap and add all five items in less than 30 seconds. Sam sets the quantity to 1 for each item without having to navigate through cluttered category menus or promotional pop-ups. Previously, checking store apps for a short mid-week run took so many taps that Sam skipped price checking altogether. Smart Shopper's fast, streamlined list input lets Sam create a short list effortlessly.

Expected Outcome

Sam builds a complete 5-item restock list in under 30 seconds and is ready to compare nearby stores immediately.

Design Considerations

  • Provide fast auto-complete search suggestions for common grocery staples.
  • Allow one-tap item addition with default quantity set to 1.
  • Keep the home list builder fast and free of unnecessary steps.
