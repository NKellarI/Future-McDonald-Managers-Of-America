Smart Shopper All Scenarios

11. Evaluating Trip Savings

Persona: Alex Morgan - Multi-Store Deal Seeker

Overall Objective

Evaluate whether the dollar savings of visiting two different stores on a shopping trip is large enough to justify the extra stop.

Scenario Narrative

On Wednesday morning, Alex enters a 12-item grocery list into Smart Shopper and reviews the trip savings summary. The app shows that buying the entire list at Kroger costs $48.20, while buying the entire list at Walmart costs $46.90. However, when Alex checks the item-by-item deal breakdown, Alex sees that buying 4 sale produce items at Kroger and the remaining 8 pantry items at Walmart lowers the combined cost to $39.40—a $7.50 additional savings. Because Alex has plenty of time on Wednesday mornings and passes both stores on the same road, Smart Shopper groups the list by cheapest store so Alex can easily buy the 4 items at Kroger and the 8 items at Walmart. Previously, Alex had to manually cross-reference flyers and write two separate paper lists to evaluate trip savings.

Expected Outcome

Alex evaluates the exact dollar savings between single-store and multi-store shopping and uses a store-grouped checklist to save an extra $7.50.

Design Considerations

  • Compare single-store totals against the combined lowest-item total clearly.
  • Allow deal seekers to group their checklist by cheapest store when splitting a trip.
  • Provide a simple, high-contrast in-store checklist view for each stop.
