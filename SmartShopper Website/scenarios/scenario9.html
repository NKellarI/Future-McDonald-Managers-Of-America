Smart Shopper All Scenarios

9. Comparing Multiple Stores

Persona: Alex Morgan - Multi-Store Deal Seeker

Overall Objective

Easily compare prices for holiday baking ingredients across multiple local stores without needing paper newspaper circulars.

Scenario Narrative

Alex is planning to bake pies and cookies for a weekend family visit and needs butter, flour, sugar, pecans, eggs, and vanilla extract. Frustrated that local grocery chains stopped mailing printed paper flyers, Alex opens Smart Shopper on a tablet. Using the clean, large-button interface, Alex types each ingredient into a list, selects Walmart, Kroger, and Target as the stores to compare, and taps "Compare Prices." Smart Shopper presents a clear side-by-side table showing the total cost of the baking list at all three stores—revealing that Kroger costs $26.10 compared to $28.40 at Walmart and $31.45 at Target—along with each store's price for every item. Previously, navigating individual grocery store websites frustrated Alex because of pop-up ads, separate logins, and tiny text. Smart Shopper compares all three stores in one distraction-free layout.

Expected Outcome

Alex compares three competing stores on one screen without paper ads or separate store accounts and saves $5.35 on baking supplies.

Design Considerations

  • Allow users to select which local stores to include in their comparison.
  • Present multi-store price tables in a clean, high-contrast, distraction-free layout.
  • Do not require users to log into individual grocery store accounts to view prices.
