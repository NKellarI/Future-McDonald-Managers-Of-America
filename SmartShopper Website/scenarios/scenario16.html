Smart Shopper All Scenarios

16. Understanding the Results

Persona: Sam Carter - Convenience-Focused Shopper

Overall Objective

Clearly understand total cost estimates, unit price comparisons (price per ounce/pound), and package size differences on the results screen.

Scenario Narrative

While reviewing comparison results for rolled oats and peanut butter on the meal-prep list, Sam notices that one store sells a 16-ounce jar of peanut butter while another store features a 28-ounce value jar on sale. To make an accurate comparison, Sam toggles the "Show Unit Price" view on Smart Shopper's results screen. The app displays the cost per ounce alongside the total package price across Walmart, Kroger, and Target, clearly showing that buying the 28-ounce jar at Walmart costs $0.11 per ounce compared to $0.17 per ounce at Target, while also noting that store totals are pre-tax estimates. Sam selects the 28-ounce jar and locks in the lower cost. Without clear unit price and estimate explanations on the results screen, different package sizes across stores make it difficult to tell which store is truly offering the better deal.

Expected Outcome

Sam easily interprets the comparison results, compares unit costs across package sizes, and chooses the best value option.

Design Considerations

  • Display clear store names, total estimates, and potential savings at the top of the results screen.
  • Allow users to view unit prices (per ounce or per pound) alongside package prices.
  • Clearly indicate that displayed prices are estimates that may vary slightly in store.
