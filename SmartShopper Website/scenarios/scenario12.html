Smart Shopper All Scenarios

12. Checking Price Information

Persona: Alex Morgan - Multi-Store Deal Seeker

Overall Objective

Verify when store prices were last updated and check unit price details so there are no surprises at the store shelf.

Scenario Narrative

Before driving across town to buy roast beef and butter that appear significantly cheaper at Kroger on Smart Shopper, Alex wants to make sure the listed prices are still current and not outdated sales from last week. Alex taps the price details icon next to the items in Smart Shopper. The app displays a timestamp showing that Kroger's and Walmart's prices were updated earlier that morning, notes that displayed totals are pre-tax estimates, and shows the unit price per pound so Alex can verify the exact package weight in the store. Once confident in the price information, Alex switches Smart Shopper into its high-contrast "In-Store Checklist" mode to check off items in the aisle. Previously, outdated third-party blogs or unclear package sizes caused Alex to distrust online price estimates.

Expected Outcome

Alex confirms that the store prices were updated today and shops with confidence using the in-store checklist.

Design Considerations

  • Display a clear "Last Updated" timestamp for store price data.
  • Include a clear notice that displayed totals are pre-tax estimates that may vary slightly in store.
  • Show package size and unit price details to ensure accurate comparisons.
