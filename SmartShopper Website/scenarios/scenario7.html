Smart Shopper All Scenarios

7. Editing the Shopping List

Persona: Jordan Lee - Family Grocery Planner

Overall Objective

Check off items while walking through the store and edit the active list when last-minute family requests come in.

Scenario Narrative

While Jordan is shopping at Walmart using an active Smart Shopper list, Jordan checks off milk, eggs, and bread as they are placed into the cart. Halfway through the trip, Jordan's spouse texts asking to pick up two jars of marinara sauce and a block of parmesan cheese. Jordan uses the Smart Shopper search bar right in the aisle to add both items to the active list and edits the marinara sauce quantity to 2. Jordan sees that Walmart's price on the marinara sauce is $0.40 cheaper per jar than Kroger's, and the app automatically recalculates the running estimated cart total so Jordan knows exactly what to expect at checkout. Before having Smart Shopper, last-minute text additions meant losing track of the running total and wondering if impulse additions were overpriced.

Expected Outcome

Jordan seamlessly adds last-minute items and quantities in the store while keeping the estimated checkout total accurate.

Design Considerations

  • Recalculate store totals automatically whenever items or quantities are edited.
  • Allow users to check off items in the aisle and move checked items to the bottom.
  • Make adding new items to an active list fast on mobile devices.
