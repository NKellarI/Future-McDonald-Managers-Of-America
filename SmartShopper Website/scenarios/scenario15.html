Smart Shopper All Scenarios

15. Returning to a Saved List

Persona: Sam Carter - Convenience-Focused Shopper

Overall Objective

Reopen a saved high-protein Sunday meal-prep list and immediately compare updated bulk quantities across stores.

Scenario Narrative

Every Sunday afternoon, Sam prepares high-protein fitness lunches and dinners for the week. Instead of building a list from scratch, Sam opens Smart Shopper and selects the saved "Sunday Meal Prep" list, which already includes 5 pounds of chicken breast, 3 cartons of egg whites, Greek yogurt, brown rice, broccoli, and bananas with preset quantities. Because Sam buys meat and dairy in bulk quantities, small price differences multiply quickly. When Sam reopens the saved list and taps "Compare Prices," Smart Shopper multiplies all item quantities and shows that Walmart totals $54.20, Kroger totals $59.85, and Target totals $64.10—a $9.90 savings for the exact same food. Previously, Sam tried tracking meal-prep prices in a manual spreadsheet, which quickly became outdated whenever stores changed their weekly prices.

Expected Outcome

Sam reopens the saved meal-prep list in seconds, refreshes current store prices, and saves $9.90 without maintaining a manual spreadsheet.

Design Considerations

  • Preserve item quantities when saving and reopening recurring lists.
  • Allow users to duplicate or slightly modify saved lists for weekly variations.
  • Automatically recalculate multiplied bulk quantities across all stores upon reopening.
