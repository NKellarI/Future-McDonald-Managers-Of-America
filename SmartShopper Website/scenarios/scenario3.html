Smart Shopper All Scenarios

3. Checking an Individual Item Price

Persona: Maya Rodriguez - Budget-Conscious Student

Overall Objective

Check individual item prices on a shopping list and swap out expensive ingredients for lower-cost alternatives to stay under a strict budget cap.

Scenario Narrative

During finals week, Maya only has $30 left in her weekly food budget. She opens her list in Smart Shopper and adds a few extra study snacks and coffee. When she taps "Compare Prices," she sees that even the lowest store total is $36.50—$6.50 over her limit. Maya taps into the individual item price breakdown to see which products are driving up the total cost. The app sorts her list by price and shows that the name-brand coffee and boneless chicken breasts account for nearly half the bill. Using the app's item search, she checks prices for store-brand coffee and chicken thighs, replacing the original items on her list. The estimated total at Walmart immediately drops to $28.15. Previously, Maya wouldn't realize she was over budget until items were being scanned at checkout, forcing her to awkwardly put items back at the register.

Expected Outcome

Maya identifies the most expensive items on her list, swaps them for cheaper store-brand alternatives, and brings her total down to $28.15.

Design Considerations

  • Allow users to view individual item prices alongside the overall store total.
  • Support sorting list items from highest price to lowest price.
  • Make replacing or swapping an item update the store totals immediately.
