Smart Shopper All Scenarios

6. Comparing the Whole Grocery Basket

Persona: Jordan Lee - Family Grocery Planner

Overall Objective

Compare the total estimated cost of a complete 25-item family grocery basket across local stores to find the best overall value.

Scenario Narrative

After updating the quantities on the "Weekly Family Essentials" list, Jordan taps "Compare Prices." With one tap, Smart Shopper refreshes the current weekly prices across Kroger, Walmart, and Target for all 25 items and their quantities. The app shows that Kroger has the lowest overall basket total this week at $118.40 compared to $131.10 at Target. Before using Smart Shopper, Jordan blindly picked a store or looked at individual sale flyers without knowing how weekly price changes affected the family's entire 25-item cart. Smart Shopper's whole-basket calculation saves Jordan both planning time and $12.70 on the weekly bill.

Expected Outcome

Jordan sees the complete basket total for each store and selects Kroger, saving $12.70 on the family's weekly trip.

Design Considerations

  • Refresh all item prices in a saved basket with a single tap.
  • Multiply item quantities automatically when computing whole-basket totals.
  • Clearly display the total cost for each store side by side.
