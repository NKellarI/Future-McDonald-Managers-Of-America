Smart Shopper All Scenarios

2. Comparing Store Totals

Persona: Maya Rodriguez - Budget-Conscious Student

Overall Objective

Find the single cheapest grocery store for a weekly list of basic staples before driving to the store.

Scenario Narrative

Once Maya finishes adding milk, eggs, bread, chicken, and bananas to her weekly list in the campus lounge, she taps the "Compare Prices" button. Smart Shopper calculates the estimated total cost for her entire list across three nearby stores, displaying Walmart at $32.41, Kroger at $35.27, and Target at $38.16, while highlighting that choosing Walmart will save her up to $5.75 on this list. Previously, Maya had to open three separate store apps and manually add items to each virtual cart just to see the total, which took so long that she usually gave up and overpaid at the closest store. Smart Shopper solves this by comparing all store totals on a single screen in seconds.

Expected Outcome

Maya immediately identifies Walmart as the lowest-cost store for her basket and saves $5.75 while staying well under her $45 weekly budget.

Design Considerations

  • Rank stores clearly from lowest estimated total to highest estimated total.
  • Highlight the maximum potential savings in a callout badge.
  • Ensure all selected item quantities are multiplied accurately in the store totals.
