Smart Shopper All Scenarios

10. Finding the Lowest Item Price

Persona: Alex Morgan - Multi-Store Deal Seeker

Overall Objective

Identify which store has the lowest individual price on expensive pantry staples like ground coffee and extra-virgin olive oil.

Scenario Narrative

Alex only needs five items this week, including a large tin of ground coffee and a bottle of extra-virgin olive oil. Because these two items are expensive on a fixed pension income, Alex wants to make sure not to overpay. Alex enters the five items into Smart Shopper and examines the individual item price differences. Even though Walmart and Kroger are within $1.00 of each other on basic produce items, Smart Shopper highlights in green that Walmart is $3.50 cheaper on Alex's preferred olive oil and $2.25 cheaper on coffee. By seeing the lowest price for each individual item clearly flagged, Alex confidently chooses Walmart for those staples and saves nearly $6.00 on a small trip. Without the app, Alex would have driven to the nearest store out of habit and overpaid for both items.

Expected Outcome

Alex pinpoints the exact store offering the lowest price on high-cost pantry staples and saves $5.75.

Design Considerations

  • Use clear visual badges (such as green highlights) to mark the lowest price for each individual item.
  • Display the exact dollar difference between the lowest and highest store price per item.
  • Allow users to filter or match identical product sizes when comparing individual items.
