Smart Shopper All Scenarios

1. Building a Weekly Shopping List

Persona: Maya Rodriguez - Budget-Conscious Student

Overall Objective

Maya wants to create a weekly grocery list of basic staples and set item quantities so she can organize what she needs before visiting a store.

Scenario Narrative

Maya is sitting in the campus lounge between her morning nursing lecture and her afternoon barista shift. She realizes she is completely out of groceries and opens the Smart Shopper app on her phone to create a new shopping list. Using the search bar, she quickly searches for and adds her five weekly essentials: milk, eggs, bread, chicken, and bananas, setting the quantity to 1 for each item. She also creates a second custom list titled "Friday Taco Night" for an upcoming dinner with her three roommates, adding ground beef, taco shells, salsa, shredded cheese, lettuce, and sour cream. Previously, Maya jotted down unorganized notes on her phone that did not track item quantities or estimated prices. Smart Shopper gives her a structured list builder that prepares her items for instant multi-store price comparison.

Expected Outcome

Maya successfully creates an organized shopping list with accurate quantities that is ready to compare across local grocery stores.

Design Considerations

  • Make searching, adding, and removing items simple.
  • Allow users to enter and adjust item quantities easily.
  • Display the entire shopping list clearly on mobile screens.
  • Provide a prominent button to transition from list building to store price comparison.
