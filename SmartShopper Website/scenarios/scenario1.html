Building a Weekly Shopping List

Persona: Maya Rodriguez - Budget-Conscious Student

Goal

Maya wants to create a weekly grocery list so she can organize what she needs to purchase before visiting a store.

Scenario

Maya is preparing for her weekly grocery trip. She opens Smart Shopper and begins adding items such as milk, eggs, bread, chicken, and vegetables. She enters the quantities she needs and reviews the completed list. After adding everything, she can compare prices across participating stores to determine where she can save money.

Expected Outcome

Maya successfully creates an organized shopping list that can be used to compare grocery prices and estimated store totals.

Design Considerations

  • Make adding and removing items simple.
  • Allow users to enter item quantities.
  • Display the entire shopping list clearly.
  • Provide an easy way to compare store prices.
