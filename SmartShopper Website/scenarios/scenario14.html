Smart Shopper All Scenarios

14. Quickly Comparing Stores

Persona: Sam Carter - Convenience-Focused Shopper

Overall Objective

Filter store price comparisons by travel distance so a quick mid-week grocery trip saves money without wasting drive time.

Scenario Narrative

With the 5-item restock list (milk, eggs, bread, chicken, and bananas) ready on a busy Wednesday, Sam taps "Compare Prices" and filters the comparison to stores within a 3-mile radius so the drive takes less than 10 minutes. Smart Shopper instantly compares the stores inside Sam's radius, showing that between the two closest stores, Kroger is $3.10 cheaper than Target, while Walmart is $5.75 cheaper overall and just 2.8 miles away. Sam heads straight to the nearby Walmart, saving $5.75 without sacrificing an hour of billable freelance work in traffic. Before using Smart Shopper, Sam had no quick way to weigh total cart savings against store proximity in a single view.

Expected Outcome

Sam compares nearby stores in seconds, chooses the best option within a 3-mile radius, and saves both time and money.

Design Considerations

  • Allow users to filter or select stores based on location or preferred local chains.
  • Display store distance alongside the estimated total cost.
  • Ensure comparison results load in a single tap after building a list.
