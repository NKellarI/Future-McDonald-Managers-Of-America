Smart Shopper All Scenarios

5. Planning a Large Grocery Trip

Persona: Jordan Lee - Family Grocery Planner

Overall Objective

Quickly update a recurring 25-item weekly family grocery list organized by category before Sunday shopping.

Scenario Narrative

On Sunday morning, Jordan has 20 minutes to plan the family grocery trip before taking the youngest child to soccer practice. Jordan logs into Smart Shopper and opens the saved "Weekly Family Essentials" list, which already contains 25 regular household items organized into categories like dairy, produce, meat, and pantry staples (milk, cereal, lunch meat, apples, and pasta). Jordan increments the quantity of milk to 2 gallons and temporarily unchecks paper towels since the family still has plenty at home. Previously, Jordan had to rewrite large paper lists from scratch every week and struggled to keep track of quantities for five family members. Smart Shopper's saved lists and category organization make managing a 25-item household trip fast and structured.

Expected Outcome

Jordan updates a 25-item family shopping list in just a few minutes without forgetting any household staples.

Design Considerations

  • Support large shopping lists of 25+ items without visual clutter.
  • Allow users to organize items into categories (dairy, produce, meat, household).
  • Let users uncheck or mute items on a saved list without permanently deleting them.
