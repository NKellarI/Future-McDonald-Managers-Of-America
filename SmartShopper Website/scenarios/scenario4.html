Smart Shopper All Scenarios

4. Saving a Weekly List

Persona: Maya Rodriguez - Budget-Conscious Student

Overall Objective

Save recurring weekly grocery lists and group meal lists so they can be reused and shared without starting from scratch.

Scenario Narrative

Because Maya buys the same basic groceries almost every week, she taps "Save List" on her staples list and names it "Weekly Dorm & Apartment Staples." Later that week, when she and her three roommates plan their "Friday Taco Night" under a $30 budget, she saves that custom list as well. After swapping an overpriced salsa for a store-brand option to bring the Kroger total down to $24.80, she uses Smart Shopper to share a text summary of the saved list and estimated total with her roommates so they know to send her $6.20 each. Previously, Maya had to re-enter the same items every week and guess how much roommates owed before shopping. Smart Shopper keeps her recurring lists organized and ready for one-tap price checks.

Expected Outcome

Maya saves her recurring staple and group meal lists for future weeks and shares accurate cost splits with her roommates.

Design Considerations

  • Allow users to save multiple named shopping lists.
  • Provide easy options to reopen, edit, or delete saved lists.
  • Include a simple export or text-sharing summary for group budgeting.
